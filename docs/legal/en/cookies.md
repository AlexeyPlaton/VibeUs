# VibeUs International Cookie & Local Storage Notice

**Version:** 2026-09-03-international-1.0

VibeUs is designed to use a small amount of browser storage necessary for authentication, security, requested product state, and user-selected preferences.

## Current storage

VibeUs may use:

- `vibeus_session` — HttpOnly account session cookie with limited lifetime;
- `vibeus_preview_session` — HttpOnly session for a particular Live Preview with limited lifetime;
- `vibus_lang` — interface-language preference in local storage;
- the selected pricing market and current workspace identifier in local storage so the requested interface state can be restored;
- short-lived project-form drafts, attribution/source values and pending promo values in session storage where used by the requested workflow; and
- temporary feedback drafts to prevent accidental loss.

A public-feedback contact value should not be persistently stored across browser sessions unless the visitor explicitly chooses a remember function.

## No advertising tracking in the current release

At this version, the canonical hosted VibeUs interface does not intentionally use third-party advertising, behavioural profiling, or marketing-tracking cookies.

VibeUs also does not currently enable optional third-party analytics in the canonical hosted release. If non-essential analytics, advertising, or similar technology is introduced, VibeUs will update this notice and implement any consent/control mechanism required before enabling that technology in jurisdictions where consent is required.

## Strictly necessary / user-requested storage

Authentication and security cookies are required to provide the signed-in service. Preference storage is used to remember choices requested by the user, such as interface language or pricing-market display.

Blocking or deleting required session storage may sign you out or prevent security-sensitive features from functioning.

## Third-party payment pages

When you leave VibeUs for a payment provider, that provider may use its own cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy/cookie terms. VibeUs does not control browser storage placed directly by an independent provider on the provider's domain.

## Changes

This notice will be updated before VibeUs intentionally introduces materially different browser-tracking practices in the hosted service.

Privacy questions: `privacy@vibeus.pro`.
