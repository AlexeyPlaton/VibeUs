## 3. Information we process

### Account and authentication

- email address;
- account and workspace identifiers;
- password hash (not the plaintext password);
- session identifiers and expiry/revocation data;
- roles and access settings;
- security and authentication events.

### Billing and entitlement

- customer email;
- country/business information supplied for checkout;
- plan, amount and currency;
- provider payment/invoice identifier and payment status;
- entitlement period;
- limited tax or business identifiers where required;
- refund/status information.

VibeUs does **not** store full card numbers or CVC/CVV data. Payment credential processing is performed by the payment provider presented at checkout.

### Product and Customer Content

Depending on enabled features:

- project names, descriptions and settings;
- feedback, comments and task content;
- page URL/route and selected-element context;
- viewport/device/browser context;
- screenshots when the relevant feature is used;
- sanitised runtime diagnostics, request identifiers, routes, exception metadata and stack information;
- integration configuration and encrypted/digested credentials where required.

By default, VibeUs is designed not to collect request/response bodies, passwords, payment-card credentials, authentication headers, or other secrets as diagnostic content.

### Support and security

- communications sent to support, privacy, legal or security addresses;
- IP address, user agent, timestamps and audit/security event metadata where reasonably required for security, abuse prevention and investigation.

- respond to support, legal and privacy requests;
- maintain records required by tax, accounting, payment, dispute or other applicable laws;

- comply with lawful requests and legal obligations.

Where VibeUs acts as processor/service provider, processing is additionally governed by the International DPA and the customer's documented instructions.

## 5. Legal bases

The legal basis depends on the applicable law and processing role. Processing may be necessary to perform a contract or take requested pre-contract steps, comply with legal obligations, protect legitimate interests such as security and fraud prevention where permitted, or rely on consent where consent is the required/selected basis.

Consent is not bundled into acceptance of the Terms where a separate consent is legally required. Optional telemetry, analytics, marketing, or external-AI data sharing must have an appropriate separate basis before activation.

## 6. Browser storage and cookies

VibeUs currently uses strictly necessary or user-requested browser storage such as:

- `vibeus_session` — HttpOnly account session cookie;
- `vibeus_preview_session` — HttpOnly Live Preview session cookie with limited lifetime;
- interface language and similar user preferences in local storage;
- short-lived draft/attribution values needed for the requested workflow.

At this version VibeUs does not intentionally deploy third-party advertising or behavioural tracking cookies in the hosted interface. If non-essential analytics or marketing technologies are introduced, the notice and consent mechanism will be updated before they are enabled where consent is required.

## 7. Hosting, localisation and international transfers

The canonical hosted VibeUs deployment is operated from infrastructure in the Russian Federation. For personal data of Russian citizens subject to Russian localisation requirements, the production architecture is intended to perform the required initial recording/systematisation/storage using databases located in the Russian Federation.

A payment provider, email provider, customer-selected integration, or other service may create a cross-border transfer depending on its location and the data flow. Such transfers are enabled only after the applicable legal and provider requirements are reviewed.

Customers must not enable an integration that creates a restricted transfer of personal information unless they have a lawful transfer mechanism and any required notices/assessments in place.

## 8. Processors and recipients

Personal information may be disclosed only as reasonably necessary to:

- infrastructure/hosting providers;
- payment providers selected for the relevant market;
- transactional email/support providers;

- professional advisers, authorities or other recipients where legally required.

The current operational list is published at `/legal/subprocessors`. A candidate provider is not represented as an active subprocessor until it actually receives production personal information.

## 9. Retention

Account and active-service information is kept while required to provide the service. After account/project deletion, active copies are deleted or de-identified within the operational periods described in the Retention Policy, subject to records that must be retained for tax, accounting, security, fraud prevention, payment disputes, legal claims, or other mandatory purposes.

Backups expire according to the documented backup cycle rather than being individually edited in place.

## 10. Security

VibeUs uses technical and organisational measures appropriate to an early-stage developer service, including authentication, role/capability controls, tenant-isolation tests, secret hashing/encryption where appropriate, audit/security logging, origin separation for Live Preview, rate limiting and automated release/security tests.

No internet service is risk-free. Customers must use their own access controls, endpoint security, backups and code review appropriate to their risk level.
