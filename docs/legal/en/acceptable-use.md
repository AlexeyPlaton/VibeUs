# VibeUs International Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 2026-09-03-international-1.0

You must not use the hosted VibeUs service, its public widget, API, CLI, MCP tools, Live Preview, tunnels, or integrations to:

- conduct phishing, credential harvesting, impersonation, or payment-page spoofing;
- make a Preview or tunnel deliberately appear to be a real bank, wallet, payment provider, government service, or other sensitive production system for deceptive purposes;
- steal or collect passwords, authentication cookies, tokens, seed phrases, private keys, full payment-card credentials, or other secrets;
- distribute malware, ransomware, spyware, malicious scripts, or intentionally harmful payloads;
- scan, attack, probe, exploit, or access systems/data without authorisation;
- bypass access controls, sanctions, provider restrictions, geographic restrictions, or security controls through false identity, residence, ownership, or business-purpose information;
- submit personal information without an appropriate legal basis or authority;
- intentionally process biometric, special-category/sensitive, payment-authentication, or similarly high-risk data where VibeUs has not expressly agreed to support that processing;
- infringe intellectual-property, privacy, confidentiality, publicity, contractual, or other third-party rights;
- use the service for unlawful surveillance, harassment, fraud, spam, or deceptive practices;
- materially interfere with service availability, including destructive testing or denial-of-service activity; or
- otherwise violate applicable law or the International Business Terms.

VibeUs may automatically restrict a public tunnel, access link, project, integration, or account where technical signals reasonably indicate abuse, phishing, malware, credential theft, or a material security threat. Where safe and lawful, VibeUs will provide a reasonable path to appeal or explain a restriction.

This policy does not restrict legitimate security testing of systems you own or are authorised to test, provided the activity does not target VibeUs infrastructure or other customers beyond the permission granted and does not violate the Security Policy.

Nothing in this policy limits rights granted by the applicable open-source licences for self-hosted code.

Report abuse: `abuse@vibeus.pro`  
Report vulnerabilities: `security@vibeus.pro`
