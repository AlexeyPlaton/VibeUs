# VibeUs International Privacy Consent Notice

**Version:** 2026-09-03-international-1.0

VibeUs does **not** treat acceptance of the International Business Terms as blanket consent for every type of personal-data processing.

Account creation, authentication, security, billing, and service delivery normally rely on contractual, legal-obligation, legitimate-interest, or other applicable legal bases described in the International Privacy Notice rather than on a single bundled consent.

Where applicable law or a particular optional feature requires consent, VibeUs will request that consent separately, in clear language, before enabling the relevant processing. Examples may include optional non-essential analytics, marketing communications, or optional transfer of Customer Content to an external AI/model provider where consent is the selected lawful basis.

A consent request should identify:

- the controller/operator;
- the specific purpose;
- relevant categories of personal information;
- intended recipients or recipient categories where material;
- material international-transfer information where applicable;
- whether the processing is optional and the consequence of refusing it; and
- how to withdraw consent.

Consent must be freely given where the law requires that standard. A user must not be forced to consent to unrelated optional processing merely to receive a core service that can reasonably be provided without it.

Withdrawing consent does not retroactively invalidate processing that was lawful before withdrawal and does not require VibeUs to stop processing that must continue under another lawful basis, for example a mandatory accounting or security obligation.
