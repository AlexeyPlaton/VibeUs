# VibeUs International Payments, Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Version:** 2026-09-03-international-1.0  
**Effective date:** 3 September 2026

This policy applies to the International Hosted Service and forms part of the VibeUs International Business Terms.

## 1. Current billing model

Paid VibeUs access is currently sold in fixed **30-calendar-day periods with manual renewal**. VibeUs does not initiate background recurring charges unless the Customer separately enables a future recurring-payment feature after the required billing information is displayed.

The price, currency, plan and access period shown immediately before checkout are authoritative for that transaction.

## 2. Eligibility and regional availability

The current international hosted launch is for business/professional customers in eligible jurisdictions. New hosted accounts and international paid checkout are **not currently offered or intentionally targeted to the EEA or United Kingdom**.

Payment methods can also be unavailable because of payment-provider, banking, sanctions, tax, or local-law restrictions. VibeUs may refuse checkout where the transaction cannot lawfully or reliably be completed.

## 3. Payment confirmation

A return to vibeus.pro from a payment page does not by itself prove payment. VibeUs grants paid entitlement only after a verified server-side payment event is matched to the expected local payment record, including provider identity and relevant amount/currency checks.

If payment is not confirmed, the paid project must not be created merely because the browser returned from checkout.

## 4. Early renewal

If a Customer purchases another 30-day period while a paid entitlement remains active, the new period is added so that unused paid time is not intentionally lost. The service ledger determines the actual entitlement dates.

## 5. Refundable situations

Subject to mandatory law and payment-provider capabilities, VibeUs will refund or otherwise correct:

- a duplicate charge for the same intended purchase;
- an amount charged above the amount presented and authorised at checkout;
- a successful payment that VibeUs cannot match to or fulfil with the purchased hosted entitlement within a reasonable remediation period;
- a payment accepted after VibeUs determines that it cannot lawfully provide the purchased hosted service to that Customer/jurisdiction; and
- any other payment where applicable mandatory law requires a refund.

For other business-customer requests, VibeUs may consider the time since payment, amount of service already delivered, technical problems, and circumstances of the request. Nothing in this policy removes a non-waivable statutory remedy.

## 6. How to request a refund

Email `support@vibeus.pro` and include:

- account email;
- workspace name or identifier if known;
- provider payment/invoice identifier if available;
- date and amount; and
- short reason for the request.

Do **not** send a full card number, CVC/CVV, online-banking password, seed phrase, private key, or other payment secret.

We may request reasonable information needed to locate the transaction and prevent fraud.

## 7. Refund method and timing

Where technically possible, refunds are sent through the original payment provider and method. Provider processing time, bank processing time, conversion and network rules are outside VibeUs's direct control.

If the original method cannot receive a refund, VibeUs will use another lawful method agreed with the Customer and supported by the provider/accounting process. VibeUs will not ask a Customer to falsify identity, residence, ownership, or transaction purpose to route a refund.

Provider fees or exchange-rate differences are handled according to applicable law and the provider's refund mechanism. VibeUs will not retain an amount that mandatory law requires to be returned.

## 8. Chargebacks and disputes

Before filing a chargeback for an ordinary support/billing problem, Business Customers are encouraged to contact `support@vibeus.pro` so the transaction can be investigated quickly. This does not waive any right to use a lawful card/payment dispute process.

Fraudulent or abusive chargebacks may lead to suspension while the payment dispute is investigated.

## 9. Cancellation

Because current international paid access uses manual renewal, cancelling normally means **not purchasing another period**. Existing paid access remains available until the current entitlement end date unless the account is suspended for a serious legal/security reason or a refund lawfully reverses the entitlement.

If VibeUs later offers recurring billing, recurring terms will not be inferred from these manual-renewal Terms. The recurring checkout must separately disclose the amount, frequency, renewal mechanism, cancellation method and any legally required notices.

## 10. Consumer purchases

The current international hosted service is not offered to consumer purchasers and is not currently offered/targeted to the EEA or UK. If VibeUs later launches consumer checkout, the consumer cancellation/withdrawal and digital-service remedies required for the relevant jurisdiction will be implemented before that launch rather than being waived by this document.
